# MacMural 1.2.0

A lightweight native macOS menu bar app for drawing over your screen. Requires macOS 13 or newer; the attached app supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

- Toggle drawing with **⌘⌥D** or **Hyper+D**, with or without Shift in your Hyperkey configuration.
- **A** for arrows, **R** for rectangles, **D** for freehand, and **L** for a fading laser while clicking and holding.
- **E** for an eraser that removes whole drawings; **⌘Z** restores everything removed in one drag.
- Hold **Space** to click and scroll underneath, then release it to resume drawing.
- **C** clears the current canvas; **⌘Z** and **⇧⌘Z** undo and redo.
- **Esc** finishes drawing and clears the session.

## Install

Download `MacMural.zip`, unzip it, and move **MacMural.app** to **Applications**. The app lives in your menu bar.

Or use the cask in this repository:

```sh
brew tap henrikhestnes/macmural https://github.com/henrikhestnes/MacMural.git
brew install --cask henrikhestnes/macmural/macmural
```

This build is ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. macOS may block the downloaded app through Gatekeeper. The cask does not disable or bypass Gatekeeper.
